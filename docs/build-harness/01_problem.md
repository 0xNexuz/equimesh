# 01. Problem — EquiMesh

## The Primary Problem

On Friday at 4:00 PM New York time, the traditional equities closing bell rings, and stock prices freeze across all Web2 brokerage systems until Monday morning. 

On-chain, however, **markets never sleep**. Tokenized equities (such as `bStocks` and `xStocks` on BNB Smart Chain) trade continuously through Saturday and Sunday, priced off references that have not updated in two days.

When weekend breaking news occurs (e.g. macro events, geopolitical shifts, or crypto market volatility), on-chain spot prices experience rapid divergence from the stale TradFi reference. 

## The Consequence

1. **Unmanaged Gap Risk:** Retail investors holding tokenized stocks over the weekend face sudden price gaps when Monday pre-market trading opens, often suffering slippage or adverse selection.
2. **Idle Capital Drag:** Capital waiting for stock pullbacks sits idle in standard wallets earning 0% yield instead of productive real-world yields (such as Ondo USDY treasuries at 5.15% APY).
3. **The Governance Vacuum:** Tokenized equities landed on-chain faster than the tooling. There are no standardized, non-custodial rebalancers or policy-governed agents that can automatically manage these baskets.

## Target Audience

- **Web3 Wealth Managers & DAOs:** Needing diversified S&P/Tech exposure on BSC without custodial counterparty risk.
- **Crypto-Native Retail Investors:** Wanting 24/7 automated exposure to US tech leaders (NVDA, AAPL, TSLA) coupled with dynamic cash yield hedging.
